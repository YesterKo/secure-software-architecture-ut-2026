== Trade-offs

=== Security and privacy vs. usability and performance

Security and privacy controls can make workflows slower or less convenient. For sensitive operations, security and privacy take priority over small improvements in convenience or response time.

=== Availability and reliability vs. cost

Higher availability and fault tolerance usually require additional resources and redundancy. Critical services should receive stronger resilience guarantees, while less critical services may accept lower availability to keep costs reasonable.

=== Auditability and transparency vs. privacy

Detailed audit information improves accountability and transparency, but collecting or exposing too much information can create privacy risks. Only information necessary for accountability should be recorded or shown.

=== Interoperability and maintainability vs. performance

Supporting different external systems and stable interfaces can add complexity and processing overhead. Interoperability and maintainability should generally take priority over small performance gains unless a workflow has strict performance requirements.
