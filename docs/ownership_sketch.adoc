= Ownership Sketch

The following are initial ownership assumptions. They do not define the final container structure.

[cols="3,4,3",options="header"]
|===
|Container/service |Responsibility |Ownership assumption

|Citizen-facing portal
|User interface, service catalogue, profile, case/status presentation, document interaction.
|Citizen Services Portal team.

|Case/workflow handling
|Coordinates portal-side request state and workflows involving external systems.
|Citizen Services Portal team.

|Identity and access
|TARA integration, portal sessions, authorization, roles, and permitted delegation.
|Portal/security team. TARA itself remains externally owned by RIA.

|External-system integrations
|Communication with KVKR, TIS, and Defence Forces systems; isolates external interface differences from the rest of the portal.
|Portal integration team. External systems remain owned by their respective organizations.

|Audit and transparency
|Records security-relevant actions and provides the information that may be exposed to citizens or auditors.
|Portal/security team.

|Notifications
|Creates and sends portal notifications through supported channels.
|Portal/platform team. Email/SMS delivery infrastructure remains externally owned.
|===
