== Mission goals

List 5-8 goals that explain what the Citizen Services Portal should achieve.

* The portal must provide value to the citizen by aggregating seperate government services onto one platform.
* The portal must keep citizen's personal data confidential from all other parties, unless permission is given for it by the citizen, or is absolutely necessary.
* The portal must provide accurate and up-to-date data to residents.
* The portal should be convenient to use for the general citizen.
* The portal should enable timely delivery of important notifications to citizens about their data and the services that they use.
* The portal should provide value to the service provider organizations by enabling quicker sharing of citizen data between service provider organizations.
* The new portal should lower the running costs compared to the old system.
