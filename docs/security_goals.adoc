== Security goals

[cols="1,4,2,2",options="header"]
|===
|ID |Security goal |Asset |Threat category

|SG-001
|Protected portal functions and data must only be accessible through a successfully authenticated identity.
|Citizen identity and session
|Spoofing / unauthorized access

|SG-002
|Citizens and staff may only perform actions permitted by their role, service rules, and any valid delegated authority.
|Delegations, cases, decisions
|Unauthorized access / privilege misuse

|SG-003
|Health, defence-obligation, case, and document data must only be disclosed to authorized users and systems.
|Health data, defence data, cases, documents
|Information disclosure

|SG-004
|Cases, documents, decisions, and other important records must not be changed without authorization, and unauthorized changes must be detectable.
|Cases, documents, decisions
|Tampering

|SG-005
|Security-relevant actions must create reliable audit records that identify the actor, action, affected resource, timestamp, and outcome.
|Audit records
|Repudiation / tampering

|SG-006
|Data exchanged with KVKR, TIS, and Estonian Defence Forces systems must only use trusted and authenticated interfaces/connections.
|External-system data and interfaces
|Spoofing / tampering

|SG-007
|A failure or compromise of one external system must not automatically grant access to unrelated portal data or services.
|Portal services and protected data
|Privilege escalation / lateral compromise

|SG-008
|Security controls must not allow excessive or malicious traffic to make critical portal services unavailable to legitimate users.
|Citizen-facing services
|Denial of service

|SG-009
|Notifications must not expose sensitive health, defence, case, or document information through insecure channels.
|Notification data
|Information disclosure

|SG-010
|Administrative and staff access must follow least privilege, and sensitive staff actions must be auditable.
|Administrative functions and protected data
|Privilege misuse / insider abuse
|===

== Trust boundaries

* Citizen device ↔ Citizen Services Portal
* Citizen Services Portal ↔ TARA
* Citizen Services Portal ↔ KVKR
* Citizen Services Portal ↔ TIS
* Citizen Services Portal ↔ Estonian Defence Forces systems
* Citizen Services Portal ↔ email/SMS notification providers
* Citizen-facing functionality ↔ privileged staff/administrative functionality

== Important data categories

* Citizen identity and session data
* Defence-obligation and case data
* Health information received from TIS
* Summonses and administrative decisions
* Uploaded and generated documents
* Delegation and authorization data
* Audit records
* Notification and contact data

== Attacker and abuse assumptions

* Unauthenticated users may attempt to access protected functionality or disrupt the service.
* Authenticated citizens may attempt to access data or actions outside their permissions.
* Staff users may accidentally or deliberately misuse privileged access.
* Expired, revoked, or prohibited delegated access may still be attempted.
* External-system credentials or integrations may be misconfigured or compromised.
* Attackers may attempt to overload public-facing services.
* Sensitive information may be exposed accidentally through logs, notifications, or interfaces.
