== Trust boundaries

Initial trust boundaries include:

* Between a citizen's browser or device and the Citizen Services Portal.
* Between the portal and the national identity provider.
* Between the portal and external agencies, registries, and back-end systems.
* Between the portal and third-party notification providers such as email or SMS services.
* Between normal citizen-facing functionality and privileged administrative or support functionality.
* Between independently operated portal components where sensitive data or privileged requests are exchanged.

== Important data categories

* Citizen profile and identity data.
* Requests, applications, and case information.
* Uploaded and generated documents.
* Delegation and authorization information.
* Audit records.
* Service-provider and registry data.
* Notification data and contact information.

== Attacker and abuse assumptions

* Unauthenticated internet users may attempt to access protected functionality, guess identities, or disrupt the service.
* Authenticated users may attempt to access information or perform actions beyond their permissions.
* A user may attempt to continue using an expired or revoked delegation.
* External systems or integration credentials may be misconfigured or compromised.
* Administrators or support users may accidentally or deliberately misuse privileged access.
* Attackers may attempt to overload public-facing services or external integrations.
* Sensitive information may be exposed accidentally through logs, notifications, or incorrectly configured interfaces.
