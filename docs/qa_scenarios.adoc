= Quality Attribute Scenarios

=== QAS-001 - External system unavailable

*Linked business goals:* Provide citizens with one trusted digital entry point for government services; improve cooperation between agencies.

*Relevant quality attributes:* Availability, Reliability & Fault Tolerance, Interoperability.

*Stimulus:* An external registry or agency backend becomes unavailable during a citizen workflow.

*Stimulus source:* External registry or agency system.

*Environment:* Normal portal operation while other external systems remain available.

*Artifact:* Portal workflows and integrations that depend on external systems.

*Response:* The failure does not make unrelated portal services unavailable. The affected workflow reports that the required external service is unavailable without losing or duplicating already submitted information.

*Response measure:* Unrelated services remain usable during the external outage. The affected workflow informs the user of the problem within `x` seconds, and no submitted request is lost or duplicated.

*Questions and issues:* It is still undecided which workflows may be delayed or resumed later and which must fail immediately.

=== QAS-002 - Revoked delegation

*Linked business goals:* Protect citizens' rights and public trust; enable lawful representation and delegation.

*Relevant quality attributes:* Security & Privacy, Auditability.

*Stimulus:* A user attempts to access data or perform an action on behalf of another person or organization after their delegation has been revoked.

*Stimulus source:* Authenticated portal user.

*Environment:* Normal operation with the delegation already marked as invalid or revoked.

*Artifact:* Authorization and delegation handling.

*Response:* The requested action is denied, protected information is not disclosed, and the denied attempt is recorded for audit.

*Response measure:* `x`% of requests using revoked delegations in the test set are denied, no protected data is returned, and an audit record of the attempt is created within `y` seconds.

*Questions and issues:* The required propagation time for delegation changes between organizations is not yet known.

=== QAS-003 - External interface changes

*Linked business goals:* Improve cooperation between agencies; reduce the long-term cost and effort required to operate, maintain, and extend digital public services.

*Relevant quality attributes:* Maintainability, Interoperability.

*Stimulus:* An integrated agency introduces a new version of its external interface or changes its data format.

*Stimulus source:* External agency.

*Environment:* The portal is already integrated with multiple agencies and existing services must continue operating.

*Artifact:* The affected agency integration and related portal interfaces.

*Response:* The changed integration can be updated without requiring breaking changes to unrelated agency integrations or portal services.

*Response measure:* Existing agency integrations continue to satisfy their current interface contracts, and no unrelated external interface requires a breaking change as a result of the update.

*Questions and issues:* The amount of implementation effort considered acceptable for an integration change still needs to be defined.

=== QAS-004 - Peak demand

*Linked business goals:* Provide citizens with one trusted digital entry point for government services; make government services easier and more convenient to access.

*Relevant quality attributes:* Scalability, Availability, Performance.

*Stimulus:* Portal traffic increases to five times the expected normal load during a national deadline or event.

*Stimulus source:* Large number of concurrent citizens and agency users.

*Environment:* Peak operation while the main external dependencies remain available.

*Artifact:* Citizen-facing portal and core request-processing workflows.

*Response:* The portal continues accepting and processing requests without widespread failures or data loss.

*Response measure:* At five times normal load, at least `x`% of common interactive requests complete within `y` seconds, the error rate remains below `z`%, and no successfully accepted request is lost.

*Questions and issues:* Expected normal and peak traffic volumes are not yet known, so the five-times-load and response-time targets are provisional and should be validated later.
