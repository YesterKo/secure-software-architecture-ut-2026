= Risk Register
:toc:

[cols="1,3,2,2,3",options="header"]
|===
|ID |Risk |Likelihood |Impact |Mitigation / treatment
|R-001
|If authorization rules for citizens, KRA staff, Defence Forces staff, or delegated access are more complex than expected, then the chosen access-control model may not support the required workflows safely.
|High
|High
|Create a role/permission matrix for representative workflows and implement an authorization PoC. Test allowed and denied cases, including revoked or prohibited delegated access.

|R-002
|If the available TIS interface, access rules, or test environment differ from our assumptions, then the health-related workflow may require major integration or architecture changes.
|High
|High
|Validate the TIS integration early with a connectivity/API PoC using available documentation, sandbox access, or representative mocks. Record unsupported assumptions and adjust the integration boundary before detailed implementation.

|R-003
|If KVKR or Defence Forces systems do not provide suitable machine-to-machine interfaces for the required workflows, then the planned portal functionality may be impossible or significantly more complex to implement.
|High
|High
|Verify the required operations and data exchanges against available interfaces. Build a small PoC integration for one representative workflow and identify where adapters, asynchronous processing, or scope changes are needed.

|R-004
|If the portal stores data that should remain authoritative in KVKR, TIS, or Defence Forces systems, then duplicated or stale sensitive data may force later redesign and create privacy risks.
|High
|High
|Define the data owner for each major data category and prototype one representative workflow using the proposed ownership boundary. Review which data is stored, cached temporarily, or fetched on demand.

|R-005
|If the portal's integration with the State Authentication Service (TARA) does not behave as expected for supported eID methods, session handling, or authentication failures, then citizens may be unable to authenticate or sessions may be handled incorrectly.
|Medium
|High
|Integrate an authentication PoC with the TARA demo environment using OIDC. Test ID-card, Mobile-ID, and Smart-ID authentication where available, together with failed authentication, session expiry, logout, and TARA unavailability. Adjust the authentication and session design based on the results.

|R-006
|If a workflow spanning the portal and external systems partially succeeds, then requests may be lost, duplicated, or shown with an incorrect state.
|High
|High
|Implement a representative multi-system workflow and inject failures at different steps. Test retries and duplicate submissions, then adjust the workflow until accepted requests are not lost or processed twice.

|R-007
|If TIS, KVKR, or Defence Forces interfaces change during development or later maintenance, then an integration may break or force unrelated portal changes.
|High
|Medium
|Add contract and regression tests for each external interface. Simulate a compatible and a breaking interface change and verify that changes remain isolated to the affected integration.

|R-008
|If audit and privacy requirements are discovered too late, then important actions may not contain enough context for traceability or the architecture may collect more sensitive data than necessary.
|Medium
|High
|Prototype audit events for representative citizen and staff workflows. Review the recorded fields against security/privacy requirements and run tests that verify required events exist without logging unnecessary sensitive data.

|R-009
|If normal and peak workload assumptions are too low, then the chosen architecture may fail to meet performance and availability targets during conscription call-up or reserve-service peaks.
|Medium
|High
|Establish a baseline workload and run load tests at normal and five-times-normal load. Measure latency, error rate, throughput, and resource use; identify bottlenecks and revise the architecture where targets are missed.

|R-010
|If realistic external-system test environments or representative test data are unavailable, then integration behavior may remain unverified until late in the project.
|Medium
|Medium
|Request test access early and define representative interface mocks for unavailable systems. Run the same contract tests against mocks and real test environments when access becomes available, and resolve any differences before release.
|===

== Notes

* Keep risk statements concrete: If condition or event happens, then impact occurs.
* Track owners and dates in repository issues or this document.
